## Exams registration and preparations guide

1. Go to https://www.aws.training/certification?refid=50fd1d88-f5af-4fa6-9b0b-5a8afc70d2f0
2. Login using the AWS Builder ID (Recommended)
4. Click Create or SignIn
5. Enter your email and password for your account
6. Verify capcha security checks.
7. Enter OTP sent to your email
8. Complete your profile details
9. Read and Accept the terms
10. Confirm your name by typing your fullname i.e First Name, Last Name etc
11. Fill the mailing address information
12. Fill in all other informations and submit.
13. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

## To register for an examination
14. Click on Exam Registration
15. Click on Schedule an exam
16. Scroll to AWS Certified AI Practitioner
17. Click on Authorize
18. Click on Schedule
19. Choose how to take the exams e.g `Online with onVUE`
20. Click `Next`
21. Choose your prefered language
22. Check all boxes and Click `Agree`
23. If asked `what is the language you prefer the proctor to speak?`, Choose `English`
24. Confirm your preferred time zone
25. Select your date from the `Calendar`
26. Select your appointment start time
27. Click `Book this Appointment`
